The rules of the heist

Everyone starts with 24 credits. Secretly bid 0–8 credits each round; 0 is a pass. All bids are spent, including losing bids. The highest positive bid wins the vault. A tie or all-pass carries the points into the next round. Unclaimed points after round 6 disappear. Most points wins; remaining credits break ties, then victory is shared. You have 30 seconds; missing bids count as passes. The host advances rounds after each reveal.
